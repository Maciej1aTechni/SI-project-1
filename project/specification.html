Technical specification of an A350 family

A350-900 (ICAO code A359)

  • Length: 66,80 m
  • Wingspan: 64.75 m
  • Height: 17,05 m
  • Fuselage width: 5,96 m
  • Max. passenger capacity: 300-350 seats*
  • Range: 15.000 km (8.000 NM)
  • MTOW**: 280.000 kg
  • Fuel capacity: 112.800 kg***

A350-900ULR (exclusive version for Singapore Airlines, ICAO code A359)

  • Length: 66,80 m
  • Wingspan: 64.75 m
  • Height: 17,05 m
  • Fuselage width: 5,96 m
  • Max. passenger capacity: 160-170 seats****
  • Range: 18.000 km (9.800 NM)
  • MTOW: 280.000 kg
  • Fuel capacity: 132.000 kg

A350-1000 (ICAO code A35K)

  • Length: 73,79 m
  • Wingspan: 64.75 m
  • Height: 17,08 m
  • Fuselage width: 5,96 m
  • Max. passenger capacity: 350-410 seats*
  • Range: 16.100 km (8.700 NM)
  • MTOW: 319.000 kg
  • Fuel capacity: 127.200 kg

A350-1000ULR (Project Sunrise aircraft, ICAO code A35K)

  • Length: 73,79 m
  • Wingspan: 64.75 m
  • Height: 17,08 m
  • Fuselage width: 5,96 m
  • Max. passenger capacity: ~238 seats****
  • Range: >19.000 km (10.300 NM)
  • MTOW: 322.000 kg
  • Fuel capacity: 143.200 kg

A350F (ICAO code A35F)

  • Length: 70,80 m
  • Wingspan: 64.75 m
  • Height: 17,05 m
  • Fuselage width: 5,96 m
  • Max. payload capacity: 111.000 kg
  • Range*****: 8.700 km (4.700 NM)
  • MTOW: 319.000 kg
  • Fuel capacity: 127.200 kg
* standard 3-class layout used by most airlines
** maximum takeoff weight
*** because of pressure changes, jet fuel is counted in kilograms instead of liters
**** Airbus had to sacrifice part of the passenger seats to make space for more fuel for longer range travels
***** at max payload
