A350 family users

  • Qatar Airways - launch customer for both A539 and A35K variants
  • Singapore Airlines - apart from normal A350, operating also 7 A359-ULR
  • Emirates - 73 aircraft from this family, used alongside iconic A380s and B777s
  • Cathay Pacific - 50 aircraft, both -900 and -1000 variants
  • Air France - one of the biggest European users, around 40 A359, replacing their A330 fleet for the newer A350
  • Delta Air Lines - one of the biggest North American users
  • Lufthansa - large, growing A350 fleet, both A359 and A35K variants as a core for long-haul operations from Munich and Frankfurt
  • British Airways - using A350 alongside B777s and B787s
  • Qantas - no A350 in service for now, 12 A35K-ULR ordered (Project Sunrise)

Airlines which ordered the new A350F

  • Atlas Air - 20, with a choice to extend the order for 20 more
  • STARLUX airlines - 10
  • Air China Cargo - 10
  • Singapore Airlines Cargo - 7, the airline wants to replace older 747-400Fs
  • Korean Air - 7
  • Etihad Airways - 7, with a choice to extend the order for more
  • Cathay Cargo - 6
  • Air France-KLM - 3 for Air France, 3 for KLM cargo
