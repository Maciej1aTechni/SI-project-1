What is the A350?

A350 is an aircraft family produced by airbus since 2006 until now. It contains 5 planes: A350-900, its longer-range brother A350-900ULR, A35-900's bigger brother A350-1000, A350-1000's longer-range brother A350-1000ULR and the youngest A350F - only cargo member in this family

Why is it so special?

A350 family members redefined long-haul travels with their quiet engines and air-trade systems. Airbus delivered 730 of them out of 1620 ordered.
